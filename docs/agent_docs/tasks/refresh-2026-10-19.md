## Derek's answers

**Group 48, the 📍 location badge on a card:** the badge keeps opening the location; **a separate ✏️ beside it moves
the spool** (the pair the spool window already has). The slicer profile on the spool window is read-only and **says
it comes from the filament**, so you know to edit it there.

**Group 49, low / out warnings:** the ⚠️ Low-stock list (per filament, a Settings threshold, default 250 g; "out" =
no stock left; Buy More links) **plus one Activity Log warning** when a print or a weigh-out drops a filament under the
threshold. He loves the print-start check ("#99 has ~34 g, this print needs ~61 g") but it needs the `.bgcode` before
it reaches the printer; he does it by hand while planning. **Later, with Project Color Loadout**, estimated from the
`.3mf`'s data.

**Group 50, the collapsed Printer Status strip:** head **numbers with a coloured ring** (cyan fine, amber under 100 g,
red empty; grams in the hover text), **no pipe** between printers; if it still doesn't fit, **grow a little: one row
per printer** when it's longer than the window (scrolling sideways is the fallback he also likes).
**"Stored, not loaded" (L290):** no notice at every print end and no "Load now" that locks him in ("I may have a few
prints I'm trying to do before I might have a chance to load it"). Instead the head's tile quietly shows a spool is
stored for it but not loaded, with a way to confirm "I loaded it". Open to other ideas, since the collapsed strip has
little room. The "reach the spool on a head" gesture stays in the oven.

**Group 37 / INDX holders:** set them up as a box with one slot per holder (no code) is "possibly the way to go", but
it's his grunt work and design first: **4 holders on each side** of the INDX (empty, a spool, or a **USS Prusa
drybox** on it); a second option, an **8-spool dryer box** whose spools all feed by tube into the printer's filament
sensor boxes (left / right on the printer, top / bottom rows in the box), still unlabelled while he decides the PTFE
routing; the **XL's holders** too, rarely used (boxes and PolyDryers), but maybe a USS drybox there later. A USS drybox
works like a PolyDryer except it fits on a holder and has no drying unit yet. → a ⏸️ PENDING DEREK item.

**Group 35, per-printer settings:** **key by the Printer row's Location ID now** (XL, RCOI). His wider point: he
needs one concrete id that carries the whole lineage (parents, children, grandparents) while keeping his naming
conventions; printers and toolheads should be joined properly. That is Group 44's sit-down. (Checked the same morning:
since the 2026-09-29 hotfix, Settings → Toolheads creates the heads' Location rows itself, `ensure_toolhead_rows`, so
